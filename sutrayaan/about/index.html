The name

Sutra means thread. Yaan means vehicle. In India's old workshops, the sutradhar held the thread that measured the work and connected the craftsmen. Sutrayaan does the same for manufacturers today: we connect what you make to the people who need to buy it.

Why we exist

India's manufacturers hold tolerances to microns, ship to Germany and Japan, and run plants that work around the clock. Yet many of their websites say little more than a phone number.

Buyers judge a supplier online before they ever call. We think the website should be as good as the factory.
