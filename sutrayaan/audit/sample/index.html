Three fixes, in order of impact

  1. 1

    Show what you make, and how well

    Why it matters. Buyers shortlist suppliers in minutes. If they can't see parts, materials, tolerances and machines, they move to the next site.

    What we'd do. A capabilities page with your machine list, materials, tolerances and industries served, plus 8–10 photos of real parts from one plant visit.

    Impact
    High
    Time
    2 weeks
  2. 2

    Replace the email address with a quote form

    Why it matters. Engineers send drawings. With only an email address, many never write, and the ones who do wait days for a reply.

    What we'd do. A quote form with drawing upload and a WhatsApp button. Every request lands in your Google Sheet with an instant alert, so nothing is missed.

    Impact
    High
    Time
    3–5 days
  3. 3

    Fix your Google Business Profile

    Why it matters. Many buyers search “CNC machining near me” and call straight from Google Maps without opening a website.

    What we'd do. Correct the category to “Machine shop”, add hours, services and 20 plant photos, match the phone number with your website, and ask five customers for reviews.

    Impact
    Medium
    Time
    2 days
