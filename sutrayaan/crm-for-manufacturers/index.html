Signs you have outgrown the sheet

  • Two or more people reply to enquiries, and nobody is sure who answered whom.
  • Quotes go out, but follow-ups depend on someone remembering.
  • Enquiries arrive from the website, IndiaMART, WhatsApp and phone calls, in different places.
  • You can't quickly answer "how many quotes did we send last month, and how many did we win?"
  • A salesperson leaves, and their customer history leaves with their phone.

Why Zoho Bigin

  • Simple. It is Zoho's CRM for small teams. It is built around a pipeline you drag deals through, not dozens of menus.
  • Affordable. Paid plans start at about half the price of Zoho's full CRM.4
  • Room to grow. If you outgrow it, Zoho supports moving up to Zoho CRM later.4
  • Indian. Zoho is an Indian company with data centres in India.

We are independent. You always pay Zoho's own list price, directly to Zoho. If we ever earn a referral fee from Zoho for your sign-up, we will tell you up front. It never changes your price.

Do I need a website from you to get the CRM upgrade?

No. We can set up Bigin on its own and connect your existing website form, IndiaMART and WhatsApp. It works best with a quote form we control, because each enquiry then arrives with full details.

Who owns the data in the CRM?

You do. The Bigin account is in your company's name and you pay Zoho directly. You can export your data any time.

Will my team actually use it?

That is why we keep it small: a few stages, a follow-up task for each enquiry, and the phone app. Training is done on the phones your team already uses.

Can it send quotes and invoices?

Bigin tracks deals and follow-ups. For quotes and invoices, Zoho has separate apps that connect to it. We can advise when you need them.

Do you work with larger manufacturers and exporters?

Yes. For larger teams we start with a paid process review, then set up Zoho CRM around how you actually sell. Setup starts at ₹75,000, and the review fee is credited if you go ahead.

What if I already use Excel or another CRM?

We import your existing customers and enquiries into Bigin as part of the setup.
