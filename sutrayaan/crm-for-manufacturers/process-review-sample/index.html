What we recommend

  1. 1

    Agree the process first

    Rules. One owner for every enquiry within 2 hours. Quotes out in 24 hours for standard parts and 3 days for drawings. A follow-up on day 3 and day 10 after each quote. A reason recorded for every lost quote.

    Price list. One approved price list for direct buyers, one for dealers, and export prices in USD and EUR, updated each quarter.

    Cost
    Your time
    Time
    1 week
  2. 2

    Put sales on Zoho CRM

    What it covers. All enquiries in one place, with website and IndiaMART leads coming in automatically. Quotes built from the price list, with approval by the sales head. Follow-up tasks created for every quote. Separate pipelines for direct, dealer and export buyers.

    For the owner. A dashboard of open quotes, win rate and expected orders, plus a Monday email report.

    Users
    6
    Time
    5 weeks
  3. 3

    Stop re-typing orders

    What we'd do. Won deals create a clean order sheet with the agreed specs, shared with production and accounts. Keep Tally for accounts for now.

    Not yet. A full ERP or Zoho One is not needed at your size today. Revisit in 12 months, once the CRM is in daily use.
