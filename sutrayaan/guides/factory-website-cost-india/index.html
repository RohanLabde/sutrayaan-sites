How much does a factory website cost in India?

By Sutrayaan · Updated 7 November 2026 · 6-minute read

Short answer: a website that actually brings enquiries to a small or mid-sized Indian manufacturer costs ₹30,000 to ₹75,000 to build, plus roughly ₹4,000 to ₹16,000 a year to run (domain, hosting and one business email, before any maintenance plan). Template sites cost less, from about ₹5,000, but rarely show what B2B buyers check. Fully custom agency builds start around ₹45,000 and can go well past ₹1 lakh.

What pushes the price up

  • Number of products. A catalogue of 200 chemicals or 80 machined parts takes longer than 10 product families.
  • Photography. A proper plant shoot adds cost, but it's the biggest single improvement most factory sites can make.
  • Languages. Each extra language (Hindi, Marathi) adds writing and checking.
  • Integrations. Connecting to a CRM, ERP or WhatsApp Business API.
  • Online selling. Payments, carts and stock are a different, bigger project.

Six questions to ask before you pay

  1. Who owns the domain? It should be registered in your company's name, not the developer's.
  2. What does it cost after year one? Ask for hosting, domain and support costs in writing.
  3. Who writes the content? If it's you, the site will take months. Good vendors write it after a plant visit.
  4. Can buyers send a drawing or spec? A quote form with file upload beats an email address.
  5. Where do enquiries go? They should reach you instantly and be tracked, not sit in an inbox.
  6. How fast is it on a phone? Ask to see a past site on your own phone, on mobile data.

Is a ₹10,000 website enough?

For a shop or a local service, often yes. For a manufacturer selling to purchase managers and engineers, usually not: cheap templates rarely show machines, materials, certifications or a proper RFQ form, and that is exactly what B2B buyers check before they call. The better question is how many lost enquiries a weak site costs you each year.

Is it better to pay once or yearly?

Pay once for the build, and separately for running costs. Avoid deals where stopping a yearly fee means losing your site or domain.

Can I keep IndiaMART and have my own website?

Yes, and most manufacturers should. Use IndiaMART for marketplace leads and your own site for buyers who search for you directly or check you before placing an order. More in our IndiaMART vs own website guide.

How long does a factory website take?

Two to three weeks for most sites, if photos and product details are ready. Large catalogues take longer.

Do I need to hire a photographer?

Not always. One planned visit with a good phone camera and daylight covers most needs; a professional shoot is worth it for export-focused sites.
