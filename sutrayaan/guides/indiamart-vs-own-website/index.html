IndiaMART catalogue or your own website: which does a manufacturer need?

By Sutrayaan · Updated 13 November 2026 · 7-minute read

Short answer: most manufacturers need both, because they do different jobs. IndiaMART brings you buyers who don't know you yet, and you share many of those leads with other suppliers. Your own website serves buyers who already have your name: from a visiting card, a referral or a quote. It checks you out, takes their drawing and sends the enquiry to you alone. Your IndiaMART catalogue page is not a substitute for a website. IndiaMART's own terms say that the web address it gives you belongs to IndiaMART, not to you.1

How IndiaMART enquiries actually work

There are two kinds, and it helps to know which one you are getting.

  • Direct enquiry. A buyer finds your product on your catalogue page and contacts you from it.2 These come from buyers who picked you.
  • BuyLead. A buyer posts a requirement on IndiaMART. IndiaMART filters it and shares it with the relevant sellers.2 You see the requirement first. Then you spend one of your BuyLead credits to unlock the buyer's contact details.6

Your plan decides how many BuyLeads you can unlock each week. IndiaMART's help page (updated March 2026) lists the following:7

One detail matters a lot. When IndiaMART reports how many enquiries it delivered, it counts each receipt of the same enquiry by multiple suppliers as a separate enquiry.8 So the buyer who messaged you is often talking to your competitors too. A fast, clear reply gives you the edge.

What IndiaMART does well

To be fair, a website can't do these things on its own:

  • Buyers from day one. IndiaMART has about 8.9 million supplier storefronts and around 2.17 lakh paying suppliers.5 Buyers come there to search. A new website has no visitors until people find it.
  • Buyers who have never heard of you. A trader in another state can find you through a category search.
  • BuyLeads. Fresh requirements arrive every week, even if no one searched for your name.

What only your own website does

  • It answers the "who are these people?" check. Purchase managers and engineers look you up before they place a serious order. They expect a proper site with your machines, materials, certifications and plant photos.
  • The enquiry is yours alone. No one else gets a copy.
  • It takes drawings. A quote form with file upload lets buyers send a drawing or spec in one step.
  • It shows up when people search your name. Google and AI assistants like ChatGPT read your site and cite it.
  • It gives you a proper email. sales@yourcompany.com looks more trustworthy than a free Gmail address on a quote.
  • It lasts. The domain, the content and the search ranking you build stay with you, whatever happens to any platform.

How to run both well

  1. Reply within minutes. Buyers on IndiaMART often hear from several suppliers, so speed counts. Turn on app notifications.
  2. Put everything in one tracker. Log IndiaMART and website enquiries in the same place. Then you can see which source brings orders, not just leads. Many CRMs can pull IndiaMART leads in automatically.9
  3. Judge cost per order, not cost per lead. Divide your yearly IndiaMART fee by the orders it actually brought you.
  4. Keep the details the same. Use the same company name, address, phone number and product names on IndiaMART, your website and your Google Business Profile.
  5. Give your website address to every buyer you win. Put it on quotes, invoices, email signatures and visiting cards. Repeat orders then come straight to you.

Is my IndiaMART catalogue page the same as a website?

No. It is a page on IndiaMART's site, in IndiaMART's template. IndiaMART's terms say that the address belongs to IndiaMART and is not permanent. A website is on a domain your company owns, and you control what it shows.

Will my own website bring as many leads as IndiaMART?

Not in the first months, and maybe never in raw numbers. It brings a different kind of lead: buyers who already know your name, and who aren't sending the same enquiry to ten other suppliers.

Can I stop paying IndiaMART once I have a website?

Only if IndiaMART orders are small compared with your direct business. Track both for six months in one tracker, compare cost per order, then decide.

Do IndiaMART leads go to other suppliers too?

BuyLeads do. IndiaMART filters each posted requirement and shares it with the relevant sellers. A direct enquiry is sent from your own catalogue page, but the buyer may still be talking to other suppliers.

What about TradeIndia and Justdial?

The same logic applies. A free listing on each is worth having. Pay only where your buyers actually search, and keep your own website as the place everything points back to.
