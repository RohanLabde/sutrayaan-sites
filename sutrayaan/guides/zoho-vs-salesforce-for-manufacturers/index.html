Zoho or Salesforce: which CRM suits an Indian manufacturer?

By Sutrayaan · Updated 14 November 2026 · 7-minute read

Short answer: for most Indian manufacturers with fewer than about 50 people using the CRM, Zoho is the better fit. It costs ₹400 to ₹2,600 per user a month, is billed in rupees, and can be live in weeks.2,3 Salesforce suits large sales teams, complex multi-country selling, or cases where a customer or parent company requires it. Its plans run from $25 to $175 and more per user a month, and it usually needs a certified partner to set up.1,9

Our interest, stated plainly: Sutrayaan sets up Zoho (Bigin and Zoho CRM), not Salesforce, and we may earn a referral fee from Zoho. We have tried to be fair, used published prices, and said clearly where Salesforce is the better choice.

The gap is widest in the middle. Zoho CRM Professional has quotes, price lists and process rules for ₹1,400. The closest Salesforce plan, Pro Suite, is $100. One Indian Salesforce partner says most Indian SMEs on Salesforce end up on Enterprise, because that is where deeper customisation starts.4

When Salesforce is the right choice

  • A large sales team. Around 100 or more CRM users, across regions or countries.
  • A customer or parent company requires it. Some global groups and OEM customers run on Salesforce and expect suppliers or subsidiaries to match.
  • Long-term supply contracts. If most revenue comes from yearly volume agreements, Manufacturing Cloud tracks planned against actual volumes and builds forecasts from them.7 It costs more than Sales Cloud. One source lists it at $275 per user a month.8
  • You have an IT team. Salesforce rewards companies that can employ an admin and keep improving it.
  • Complex, unusual processes. If you need heavy custom logic and many system integrations, Salesforce has the deeper platform.

When Zoho is the right choice

  • Fewer than about 50 CRM users. This covers most Indian manufacturing SMEs.
  • You want it live this quarter. A small team can be working in Bigin within a week.
  • Budget matters. The yearly Salesforce licence for 10 users can pay for Zoho for several years.
  • Nobody on staff will be a full-time CRM admin.
  • You want rupee billing and one family of apps. Zoho's CRM, accounts and inventory apps come from the same company.
  • Your team works on phones. Both have mobile apps. Bigin is simple enough for a sales team that has never used a CRM.

Five questions to ask before you choose either

  1. How many people will log in every day? Pay for those, not for everyone.
  2. What is the full first-year cost? Add licence, setup, training and GST. Ask for it in writing.
  3. Who will look after it after go-live? If the answer is "nobody", pick the simpler tool.
  4. Is our process clear? Software won't fix an unclear process. Agree the steps from enquiry to order first.
  5. Can we get our data out? Check that you can export customers, deals and quotes at any time.

Is Zoho good enough for a manufacturing company?

For most small and mid-sized manufacturers, yes. It handles enquiries, quotes, price lists, approvals, dealer and export pipelines, and owner reports. Very large teams or contract-heavy businesses may need Salesforce.

Why is Salesforce so much more expensive?

It is built for large companies. The licence is priced in dollars, the plans most firms need start at $100 per user a month, and setup usually needs a certified partner.

Can we start on Zoho and move to Salesforce later?

Yes. Both let you export your customers and deals. A clear process and clean data matter more than the first tool you pick.

Do both work with IndiaMART and WhatsApp?

Both can receive IndiaMART leads and connect to WhatsApp, through built-in features or connector tools. Check what your plan includes before you buy.

Is there a free version of either?

Zoho CRM is free for up to three users, and Bigin is free for one. Salesforce also lists a free tier with limited features. Free plans suit a trial, not a sales team.
