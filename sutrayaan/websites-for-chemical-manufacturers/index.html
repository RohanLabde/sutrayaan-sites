Questions

Asked by chemical company owners.

Should we publish COAs and SDSs openly?

Usually not. We show that they're available and let buyers request them, so you know who's asking and can follow up.

We have 200+ products. Is that a problem?

No. We load the catalogue from your spreadsheet, so adding products later is a simple update.

Can you show CAS numbers without revealing our customers?

Yes. The catalogue lists what you make and at what grade, never who buys it.

Do export buyers really check the website?

Yes. A foreign buyer's first screen is usually your site: certifications, product list and a working contact route.
