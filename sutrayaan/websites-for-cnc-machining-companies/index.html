What buyers check first

An engineer decides in two minutes.

Purchase and engineering teams shortlist machine shops on their phones before they ever call. This is what they look for.

1

Machines and capacity

Turning, VMC, 5-axis, grinding: models, bed sizes and the largest part you can take.
2

Materials and tolerances

The grades you machine (SS 304/316, EN8, EN19, brass, aluminium) and the tolerance you hold.
3

Quality proof

ISO 9001 or IATF 16949 with certificate number, inspection equipment, and a sample inspection report.
4

Parts you've made

Photos of real components by industry: automotive, pumps, valves, hydraulics, textile machinery.
5

A way to send a drawing

An RFQ form that takes PDF, STEP and DWG files, with an NDA option.
6

Fast, clear contact

A named person, a WhatsApp button, and a promise of when they'll hear back.

Questions

Asked by machine shop owners.

Can buyers upload drawings safely?

Yes. On your site, drawings are saved privately to your company's Google Drive and linked to the enquiry, and buyers can tick a box to ask for your NDA first. Nothing is visible on the public site.

We do job work for big OEMs under NDA. What can we show?

We show capability, not customer parts: machine list, materials, tolerances, generic sample components and industries served. You approve every photo.

Do we need a website if we're on IndiaMART?

IndiaMART brings small enquiries. OEM buyers and exporters check your own website before they send a drawing. Keep both and link them.

How do you know what to write about machining?

We visit your plant once, see the machines and the parts, and write it in your terms. You review every word before it goes live.
