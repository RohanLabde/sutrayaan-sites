Questions

Asked by box maker owners.

Should we show prices on the website?

Showing a starting price per box size builds trust. We usually suggest 'from' prices on the site, with the exact price quoted within a day.

We already get orders on IndiaMART. Why a website?

Brands and larger buyers check your own site before committing to a regular supplier. It also stops your enquiries being shared with competitors.

Can buyers upload their artwork?

Yes. The pricing form accepts artwork and dieline files along with the size and quantity.

Do you cover printed mono cartons too?

Yes. We add any product line you make, from corrugated boxes to mono cartons and rigid boxes.
