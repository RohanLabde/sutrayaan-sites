What buyers check first

OEM buyers check fit before they call.

A buyer with a new part needs to know quickly whether your plant can make it. This is what they look for.

1

Press range by tonnage

Clamping force from smallest to largest, number of machines, and shot weight.
2

Tool room

Whether you design and build moulds in-house, and how fast you get to T1 samples.
3

Materials you mould

PP, ABS, PC, nylon, glass-filled grades and engineering plastics you run regularly.
4

Secondary operations

Insert moulding, ultrasonic welding, printing, assembly and packing.
5

Quality systems

ISO 9001 or IATF 16949, CMM or measurement setup, PPAP experience if you supply auto.
6

A quote form that asks the right things

Part drawing, material, annual volume, and whether a mould exists or needs building.

Questions

Asked by moulding owners.

Should we show our press list publicly?

Yes, as a range and count. It's the first thing OEM buyers check, and it filters out enquiries you can't run.

We make moulds too. Can the site cover both?

Yes. Tooling and moulding get their own sections, and the quote form asks whether the buyer needs a new mould, has one, or wants one modified.

Our customers are confidential. What photos can we use?

Generic parts, the shop floor, the tool room and machines, all approved by you. No customer logos unless you have permission.

Will it help with export buyers?

Yes. The site is in clear English, loads fast abroad, and shows the facts export buyers screen for: certifications, capacity and materials.
